# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Concept and prototype (now)
- Static HTML prototype in `prototype/`
- Concept, XP rules, data model
- Supabase schema and RLS

## Phase 1 — MVP: log and level
- Auth, cat profiles (Milo, Gipi)
- Quick log bottom sheet: feed, water, litter, play, grooming, weigh
- Care XP, cat level, owner level
- Daily Quest and Care Streak
- Weight chart with milestones

## Phase 2 — Passport and journal
- Health records with due dates and reminders
- Health Passport page and Export Vet Report PDF
- Photo journal with Storage
- Health Timeline (story view)

## Phase 3 — Personality, relationship, insights
- Traits and trait suggestions from data
- Milo × Gipi relationship score and socialization timeline
- Rule-based anomaly detectors (weight velocity, appetite vs 30-day average, weight flat with normal intake)
- Weekly summary

## Phase 4 — Life story
- On This Day
- AI summary on top of detectors
- Achievements polish, sharing a profile card as an image
